# Character Army Builder

A party game for 2 to 4 players. Bid on a random roster of characters from history, fiction and real life, then see how your team handles a random challenge.

Play it at https://donadavs.github.io/Character-Army-Builder/

- **Play online:** one person hosts and shares a link or room code; everyone plays on their own device.
- **Auction:** 20 seconds per character, and a bid in the last 5 seconds resets the clock to 5. No team size limit.
- **Bots:** fill empty seats with up to 3 bots that value characters differently and spend their spare cash late.
- **Challenge:** argue your case, then everyone votes a 1st and 2nd pick. Second picks break ties.
